1. Optimise the topic images

This is the most important one.

If Topic1.jpg, Topic2.jpg and so on are large files, they will slow the page more than anything else.

For index-page cards, you do not need huge images. A sensible target is usually:

around 500 to 800 px wide
compressed JPG
ideally well under 150 KB each, and preferably lower if quality still looks good

This can make a very noticeable difference.




Always crop images to 4:3 ratio (matches your card)
Example sizes:
800 × 600
1200 × 900
