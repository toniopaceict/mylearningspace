GLIP v419 Support Role

Built directly from the supplied v418 Apps Script and GitHub baselines.

Role
- Internal value: support
- Display label: Support
- Stored in Teachers and assigned through TeachingAssignments.
- Support is staff for login purposes but uses the learner-facing interface.

Teaching assignments
- Support rows retain class_id, curriculum_id, teacher_id, active and archived.
- student_work_folder_id and resources_folder_id remain blank.
- No Drive folders are created or repaired for Support assignments.

Access
- Subjects and topics come from active TeachingAssignments.
- Class Resources are resolved from an active non-Support assignment with the same class_id and curriculum_id.
- Support sees the learner-style Class Resources interface and can download resources.
- Student submissions and progress still require a real student identity; server-side validation remains authoritative.

Architecture
- No new sheets or columns.
- Existing storage architecture is unchanged.
- New central helpers distinguish student-like and teacher-like users.
