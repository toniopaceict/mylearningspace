ADR-019: Teaching-role workflow refactor

Status: Accepted
Asset version: 382

Decision
--------
Subject Teacher and Lead Teacher behaviour is now coordinated by
js/teaching-role-context.js.

The shared module owns:
- teaching-role detection
- normalised teacher permissions
- active-assignment detection
- capability checks
- subject-and-level permission checks
- the common teaching-role navigation
- teaching-page access guards

Role differences are expressed as capabilities rather than repeated role-name
checks. Lead Teachers can manage topics. Subject Teachers cannot. Both roles can
view subjects and class resources, manage their assigned folder links and access
the future Progress area.

Management and owner workflows remain unchanged. Owner and Admin access to Topic
Management continues through the existing administrator route.

Reason
------
The previous implementation repeated teacher-role and permission logic in the
header, Subjects page and individual teaching pages. Centralising that logic
reduces drift and makes future teaching-role changes safer.
