GLIP ADR-020: Topic Activity Cache Coherence

Decision
--------
Topic and activity navigation uses cached data for the immediate first paint,
but every topic page then performs an authoritative live refresh. The refreshed
context rebuilds the navigation menu without requiring a hard refresh.

Activity add/edit operations invalidate the relational snapshot explicitly.
Direct edits to watched relational sheets invalidate the snapshot through
onEdit. Fresh topic-page requests also bypass the snapshot and read the live
sheets, covering structural row deletions that may not fire a simple onEdit
trigger.

Asset version: 384.
